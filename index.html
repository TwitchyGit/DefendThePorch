Get Off My Lawn

They just keep coming to the door — dogs, cats, the postman, nosy neighbours, delivery drivers, politicians, drones, even a delivery truck. Not today.

Move between the 3 walkways with ↑ / ↓ (or W/S), and shift a step with ← / → (or A/D) to reach items beside the porch.

Pelt intruders with whatever's in your purse using Space or Left Click. Switch weapons with 1 (Candy), 2 (Rolling Pin), 3 (Teacup) — hitting an enemy with the Teacup or a special item gives it a red hue and slows it down.

Watch for ✨ sparkly pickups — small ones are quick and weak, big ones like a Watermelon or Garden Statue hit hard but take a beat to throw. The Smelly Sock and Frisbee fly clean through the whole lane, knocking everyone in their path into retreat and taking out two or more at once. Find a Bin Bag and it's game over for that lane — she turns, sprints right off-screen, and flattens everything in her path before dashing back. Sometimes several pickups appear across different lanes at once — grab one before it fades, then press 4 to unleash it.

Get caught next to an enemy while out of position and you'll be startled back to the porch! Don't let anyone reach the door!
