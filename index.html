Get Off My Lawn

They just keep coming to the door — dogs, cats, the postman, nosy neighbours, delivery drivers, politicians, drones, even a delivery truck. Not today.

Move between the 3 walkways with ↑ / ↓ (or W/S), and shift a step with ← / → (or A/D) to reach items beside the porch. On a phone: swipe up/down or left/right, tap to throw, and use the buttons on screen to switch weapons.

Pelt intruders with whatever's in your purse using Space or Left Click. Switch weapons with 1 (Candy), 2 (Rolling Pin), 3 (Teacup) — the Teacup and special items slow enemies with a red hue.

Watch for ✨ sparkly pickups. Small ones are quick and weak; big ones like the Watermelon or Garden Statue hit hard. The Smelly Sock and Frisbee fly through the whole lane, taking out two or more intruders. Grab a Bin Bag and she charges off-screen, flattening her lane. Watch out for the Clone Swarm to destroy all intruders. Find a Mystery Vegetable and unleash a vegetable hailstorm. Grab any item before they disappear and press 4 to unleash them.

Get caught next to an enemy while out of position and you'll be startled back to the porch! Don't let anyone reach the door!
